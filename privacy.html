SaveWare Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

SaveWare is developed by CentWare. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the SaveWare app and website. The short version: we do not collect your personal data.

Your data stays on your device

Everything you enter into SaveWare — your debts, budget, subscriptions, and savings goals — is stored locally on your own device (in the app's local storage). It is never sent to CentWare, never uploaded to our servers, and we have no way to see it. We do not operate servers that receive your financial information.

No accounts, no tracking

SaveWare has no user accounts, no sign-up, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking. We do not collect names, email addresses, or usage statistics from the app.

Internet access

The app uses an internet connection only to load the SaveWare web application itself (hosted on GitHub Pages) and a font stylesheet (Google Fonts). Those services may see your device's IP address as part of normal web delivery, the same as loading any web page. No information you type into SaveWare is included in those requests.

Export and import

If you use SaveWare's Export feature, the file is created on your device and goes only where you choose to send or save it. Import reads a file you select. Neither action transmits your data to CentWare.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
